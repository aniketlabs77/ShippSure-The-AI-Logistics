How vehicle matching works

We compare your actual weight against the volumetric weight of your parcel (L×W×H in cm, divided by 5000) and route it to the smallest vehicle class that comfortably fits.

  • Two Wheeler — up to 20 kg
  • Mini Truck — up to 750 kg
  • Van — up to 1,000 kg, bulkier loads
  • Container Truck — industrial / palletized freight

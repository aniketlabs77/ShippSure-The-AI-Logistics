The AI engine

Two models, one manifest

Every booking runs through a vehicle-matching model that reads weight and dimensions, and every tracking page is narrated by a summarization model that turns raw checkpoints into a plain-language brief.

Vehicle prediction

Weight + volumetric size (L×W×H ÷ 5000) is compared against four vehicle classes, from two-wheeler to container truck, and returned with a confidence score.

Status summarization

Booking, pickup, transit and delivery events are condensed into a two-line brief so customers don't have to read a raw event log.

Process

From booking to doorstep

01 / BOOK

Customer enters pickup, drop and goods details — AI recommends the vehicle instantly.

02 / ASSIGN

An approved driver is matched to the shipment automatically.

03 / TRACK

Driver updates location as the load moves; AI narrates the status for the customer.

Driver marks delivered and uploads proof — the manifest closes out.
